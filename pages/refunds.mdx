## Purpose & Overview

SameDayCustom (SDC) is committed to maintaining strong customer satisfaction while supporting our valued print partners. This policy outlines the expectations and processes for how refund-related issues should be handled in coordination with partner stores.

## Vendor Responsibilities

Vendors are expected to:

- Produce orders accurately and on time.
- Update product listings (availability, colors, sizes) regularly.
- Notify SDC of any production issues or delays as soon as possible.
- Ensure order hand-off to delivery companies is prompt and recorded properly.
- Maintain communication with SDC when issues arise.

## Identifying Responsibility

When a refund or complaint is initiated, SDC will determine the cause of the issue. Vendor payout may be impacted only if the issue is traced back to the partner store.

- **Vendor Fault** includes:
  - Poor print quality (e.g., peeling, color issues, resolution)
  - Incorrect item/design/size printed
  - Production or driver request delays
  - Closing the store early without prior notice
  - Outdated product listing causing unfulfillable orders
- **SameDayCustom Fault** includes:
  - System bugs or design tool issues
  - Design errors created by SDC
  - Miscommunication or misassignment
- **Customer Fault** includes:
  - Providing incorrect or low quality artwork
  - Misspellings or incorrect instructions
  - Inaccurate delivery information
- **Courier Fault** includes:
  - Lost or delayed packages
  - Damaged packages during delivery

## Refunds & Redos

- If the vendor is at fault, SDC may reduce the partner payout to cover customer compensation or ask for order redo.
- SDC will notify the vendor of any payout adjustments along with reasoning.
- If the issue is due to SDC or a courier, vendor payout will typically remain unaffected.
- In some cases, SDC may request a redo. Vendors are expected to prioritize such redos when requested.

## Order Cancellations (Pre-Production)

If a customer requests cancellation before production begins:

- SDC will attempt to retain the order via timing/design adjustments.
- If the order is still cancelled, the vendor will not be penalized.

## Customer-Provided Garments

If customer-supplied garments are accepted, vendors agree that:

- They are not liable for replacing the garment if it is damaged during production.
- Liability is limited to the decorating fee only.

## Copyrighted Content

If an order includes potentially infringing content:

- Vendors must pause production and notify SDC.
- The customer will be asked to provide a signed waiver or updated design.
- If the customer cannot resolve the issue, the order may be cancelled and refunded at SDC's discretion. Vendor payout may still be honored if production has already occurred.

## Disputes and Communication

If there is a dispute between a customer and the vendor without clear proof of fault:

- SDC will make the final decision.
- SDC may absorb the cost to preserve the vendor relationship.

## Contacts and Support

For clarification or to report an issue, please contact our support team through our platform.
