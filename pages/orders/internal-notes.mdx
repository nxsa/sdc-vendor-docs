We developed a note-taking system that allows store locations to write notes about an order. Notes can be left as text, images, or other file types.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Internal Notes">
    To add a note, simply click on **View Internal Notes** on the left panel of the orders page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/notes/view-internal-notes-btn.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click on the input field">
    Click on the input in the Internal notes panel.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/notes/internal-notes-input.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add and save the note">
    Start typing your note and click **Save note** when done. You can also add images and files.

    <Frame caption="Add your internal note here">
      <img src="/images/orders/notes/add-internal-note-modal.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Example of an internal note">
      <img src="/images/orders/notes/example-internal-note.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
