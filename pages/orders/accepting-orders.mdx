For every new order that is assigned to your store, you will receive the following notifications:

1. An email message.
2. A browser notification.
3. An automated phone call to the store, when there is a delay in accepting the order.

When orders are assigned to a store, they appear on the orders table with a status of **New Order** as shown below;

<Frame caption="New order on the orders table">
  <img src="/images/orders/accepting/new-order-table.png" />
</Frame>

Clicking on the order will open the order details. You must accept new orders as soon as possible to start processing them.

- **Quickly accepting orders** increases your rating as a vendor which will enable us to prioritize you for all future orders.
- **Declining orders** often results in disconnecting your store from the platform except for extenuating circumstances such as: equipment breakdown, the store being closed for that day, or the customer requesting it.
- **Order Notes & Messages**: Make sure you pay attention to all notes and messages from the customers.

<Note>To accept new orders, click the **Accept** Button on the order details</Note>
