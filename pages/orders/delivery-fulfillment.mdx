## Recording a Delivery

To record a delivery, simply click the **Ready for pickup** or **Prepare Shipment** button on the top right of the page.

<Frame caption="Record a delivery by marking the order as ready for pickup or preparing a shipment">
  <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/record-delivery-btn.png" />
</Frame>

<Note>Delivery can be recorded on all the items in an order or only part of the order can be recorded for delivery.</Note>

When a store location marks production complete on all the items in an order, the system will automatically notify you to either inform the customer that the order is ready for pickup (if it's a pickup order) or record shipping for shipping orders.

<Frame caption="Notify customer to pickup the order or prepare a shipment for delivery">
  <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/notify-or-prepare.png" />
</Frame>

If you wish to change the method of delivery for the customer, you can simply click **Record with a different method**.

You can record a partial fulfillment of an order. To do this, simply uncheck **Deliver all items** and manually enter the delivering quantities for each size in a line item in an order.

<Frame caption="Record a partial or full fulfillment of an order">
  <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/partial-fulfillment.png" />
</Frame>

## Pickup Delivery Method

To record a pickup delivery method, simply confirm or edit the **Recipient's name** and **Phone number**. You can leave a note for the customer if you want to.

If you are only confirming the customer’s information, you can click **Skip** to proceed.

<Frame caption="Record a Pickup Delivery Method">
  <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/recipient-info.png" />
</Frame>

### Pickup Confirmation

The customer must sign a pickup confirmation slip at the pickup time. This is to enable us to verify that the order has been picked up by the customer.

The store will upload the signed copy of the pickup confirmation slip back to the system by scanning the slip and uploading it or by taking a picture of the signed slip with any smartphone and scanning the QR Code on the pickup details page.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/pickup-confirmation.png" />
</Frame>

### Downloading a blank pickup confirmation slip

You might want to have copies of the pickup confirmation slip available for ease of use. You can get that by clicking **“More”** on the menu sidebar and then downloading the pickup confirmation slip. You can download as many copies as you wish.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/download-blank-slip.png" />
</Frame>

## Shipping Delivery method

To record a shipping delivery, you must submit the following;

- Shipping Date
- At least one package with this information;
  - Package Dimensions (Length, width, and height)
  - Package Weight (which you can override to enter a more accurate weight)

You can also require a customer's signature from the delivery courier.

You can submit more than one package.

<Note>If you have more than one package with the same dimensions and weight, then simply increase the **Number of Boxes** instead of adding a new package.</Note>

<Frame caption="Recording a shipping delivery">
  <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/shipping-delivery.png" />
</Frame>

<Note>Please verify and confirm the delivery address before clicking proceed.</Note>

## Car Delivery Method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a Driver">
    Click **Request a Driver** to prepare the delivery for the customer.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/car-delivery-request.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm quantities and method">
    Confirm the quantities you're delivering and click **Proceed**. Make sure to confirm the delivery method.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/car-delivery-confirm.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Package Name and proceed">
    Make sure under "Package Name" the option "Bag" is chosen if you're using a bag, amend the details, then click on "Proceed".

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/car-delivery-package.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record shipment details">
    Shipment details will appear. Click on **Record** to proceed.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/car-delivery-details.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download Packing Slip">
    You will be directed to the following page where you can download the Packing Slip.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/car-delivery-download-slip.png" />
    </Frame>

    When you download the Packing Slip, you will get a document similar to the one below. The Packing Slip includes a tracking link to track the order.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/car-delivery-packing-slip-doc.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the delivery">
    To track a delivery, click on **Delivery Record** as shown below.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/car-delivery-record-tab.png" />
    </Frame>

    A side window will open up, click on **Track Delivery** to track the order.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/car-delivery-track-btn.png" />
    </Frame>

    You will get the following window to allow you to track the order.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/car-delivery-tracking-map.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>There may be more than one attempt before a driver accepts the order. If the system keeps saying finding a driver that is fine. Sometimes the system finds a driver then it cancels because the driver refused the order.</Note>

<Note>ONLY the store can call the driver using their number, we are unable to call the driver using our number.</Note>

## Deleting a Delivery Record

If for some reason, you need to cancel a delivery, you can do that by:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Delivery Record">
    Open the order details page and click on the **Delivery Record** tab.

    <Frame caption="Deleting a Delivery Record">
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/delete-delivery-record-tab.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the Delete icon">
    Click the **Delete** icon to delete the delivery record.

    <Frame caption="Deleting a Delivery Record">
      <img src="/images/orders/fulfillment/delete-delivery-record-icon.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Please be aware that you cannot delete a delivery record for a delivery that has been fulfilled.</Note>
