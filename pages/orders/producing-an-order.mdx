---
title: "Producing an order"
description: ""
---

Once the artwork is approved, it will go into the production stage. You will need to mark them as Produced.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Mark as Produced">
    To start the production status update, click the **Mark as Produced** button at the top right of the order details page.

    <Frame caption="Mark as Produced button">
      <img src="/images/orders/production/mark-as-produced-btn.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose production mode">
    You can either mark the order as:

    - **Fully Produced** — when you have finished printing all the quantities in the order.
    - **Partially Produced** — if you're dealing with a big order and you want to keep track of what you're doing (so you don't overproduce an order), or you agreed with the customer to deliver the order in batches.

    <Frame caption="Marking an order as fully produced">
      <img src="/images/orders/production/fully-produced-option.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Marking an order as partially produced">
      <img src="/images/orders/production/partially-produced-option.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit sizes and quantities (for Partial Production)">
    When you mark an order as **Partially Produced**, you can submit the sizes and quantities of any product in the order that has been produced.

    <Frame caption="Submitting sizes and quantities of products produced">
      <img src="/images/orders/production/partial-production-quantities.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>
