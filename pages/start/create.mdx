After you’ve been approved on the platform, you need to create/setup your store so
you can begin receiving orders that will be assigned to you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Locations & Settings">
    To create your first store, navigate to **Locations & Settings** by clicking **More** on the sidebar.
    <Frame>
        <img src="/images/createstore/create-1.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a location">
    Click **Add a Location** to create a store.

    <Frame caption="You can add a single store or add multiple store locations at a time">
        <img src="/images/createstore/create-2.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>To add multiple locations at a time, simply download the CSV Format, fill in as many stores as you want to add and upload to the system</Note>
