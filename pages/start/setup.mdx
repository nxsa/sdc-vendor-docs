After you've finished creating your store, you will need to set up the following before you can be eligible to receive orders:
- Work hours
- Products & pricing
- Delivery Methods

<Note>These are important because without them, the admin will not be able to assign orders to the store</Note>

To go back to the store setup, navigate to **Locations & Settings** and click on the gear icon for the store you want to setup/change settings.

<Frame caption="Finish setting up the store">
  <img src="/images/setup/setup-1.png" />
</Frame>

<Steps>

  <Step title="Store Profile">
    You can edit change some information about the store location from here. You cannot change the store address. You'd need to contact the SameDayCustom Admin to do that if you want to.

    <Frame caption="store profile">
      <img src="/images/setup/store-profile.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>To use the webshop feature, please get in touch with the SameDayCustom Admin</Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="General">
    **Auto accept all orders**

    When this is enabled, all orders that are assigned to a store location will be automatically accepted by the store.

    **Disable defer orders**

    When this is enabled, you will not be able to add delay to the order processing time.

    <Frame caption="general">
      <img src="/images/setup/general.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Work Hours">
    **Regular hours**

    Configure the days your store will be open and the opening and closing hours for each day you activate.

    <Frame caption="Regular hour setup">
      <img src="/images/setup/work-hours-regular.png" />
    </Frame>

    **Holiday hours**

    With holiday hours, you can set specific days when your store will be closed, such as national holidays, even if those days normally fall within your regular operating schedule. You can also customize your store's opening and closing hours for any given day to better match your holiday plans.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/setup/work-hours-holiday-1.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/setup/work-hours-holiday-2.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/setup/work-hours-holiday-3.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Payment Settings">
    This enables us to keep records of payouts made to stores for tax purposes.

    <Frame caption="Payment settings">
      <img src="/images/setup/payment-settings.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Delivery Method">
    You can enable the delivery methods available for this store location here.

    <Frame caption="Delivery Method">
      <img src="/images/setup/delivery-method.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Notifications">
    Choose the types of notifications store-assigned users will receive, and specify if they should be sent by SMS, email, or both.

    <Frame caption="Notifications settings">
      <img src="/images/setup/notifications.png" />
    </Frame>


     <Frame caption="Assign Notifications">
      <img src="/images/setup/assign-notifications.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Printing Methods">
    This allows you specify what product printing methods you offer at your store location.

    <Frame caption="Printing methods setup">
      <img src="/images/setup/printing-methods.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Products & Pricing">
    You can configure products, sizes and colors for the inventory in your store from this page.

    There are certain rules to setting up a product that you must know;

    - You cannot set a product as available unless you set at least one color as available.
    - You also cannot set a color as available unless you set at least one size, under that color, as available.

    <Note>All store locations must carry the **White Adult Unisex Crewneck Short Sleeve T-shirt**. Each newly created store location will have the product and color added to their store as a default.</Note>

    - To add a product simply click on the **Add a product** button.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/setup/products-pricing.png" />
    </Frame>

    - A modal will be shown to you where you can search for a product. Click the checkbox on each row for the products you want to add. Click **Add selected products** when you are done.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/setup/add-product.png" />
    </Frame>

    - Add a product does not immediately make it available on your store. You have to add colors and sizes for each product then make the product available. Click **Manage** on a product to add colors and sizes.
    - Clicking **Edit** on a color will allow you to add sizes to that color and make the color available for sale.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/setup/manage-product.png" />
    </Frame>

    - To add a size to a color, simply click **Available** on the sizes you have in stock. When you are done, click the dropdown of the **Color Status** and set the status to Available. Click **Save changes** when you are done.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/setup/edit-color.png" />
    </Frame>

    - To make a product available in a store, you have to set the product as available. Click **Edit product organization** on the right panel of the page. You will be shown a modal, click the dropdown, select Available, and click **Save Changes**. This will set the product as available and make it visible when a customer visits your store.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/setup/edit-product-org.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/setup/product-status-modal.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

</Steps>
