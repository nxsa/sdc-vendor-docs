<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the platform">
    Visit the platform by typing
    [partner.samedaycustom.com](https://partner.samedaycustom.com) in your
    browser - Click on <u>**Log in**</u> on the top right corner of the page.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/login/imgLogin.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter your email address and password you used to register on the platform
    and click **Log in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on notifications">
    Accept notifications for the platform (or enable it if you didn't accept initially), so you get alerts for all system notifications.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/login/permissions.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>
