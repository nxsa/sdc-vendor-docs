Roles & Permissions are a set of rules that define the access a user has to parts of the vendor account.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/user-management/roles-and-permissions-list.png" />
</Frame>

### General permission rules are:

- Accept Orders
- Business Profile
- Dashboard
- Locations
- Messages
- Operations
- Payment Settings
- Reports
- Settings
- Store Profile Settings
- Users And Permissions

Each permission rule contains a list of detailed permission access that can be granted or revoked. Checking an item on the detailed permissions list will grant access to the role, and unchecking will revoke access.

<Frame>
      <img src="/images/user-management/create-role-btn.png" />
</Frame>


There are two default roles in the system:

- **Manager** — The Manager role has full access to all parts of the system.
- **Operations** — The Operations role only has access to the operations and messaging parts of the system.

<Note>You can create additional Roles and assign permissions to the role.</Note>


To create a role, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create role">
    Navigate to the **Roles & Permissions** page and click **Create role**.

    <Frame caption="Setup role">
      <img src="/images/user-management/setup-role.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the role details">
    - Enter the name of the Role in the **Role Name** input.
    - Enable the permissions you wish to assign to that role.

    <Frame caption="Configure role details">
      <img src="/images/user-management/configure-role.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** when you’re done to finalize the new role.
  </Step>
</Steps>
